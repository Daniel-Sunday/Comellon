---
name: grilling
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrase.
---
- Interview relentlessly about every aspect of the plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.
- For each question, provide your own recommended answer — don't just ask open-ended.
- Ask questions **one at a time**, wait for feedback before continuing. Multiple questions at once is bewildering.
- If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead of asking me.
